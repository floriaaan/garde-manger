Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: one high-fidelity portrait mobile app auth welcome screen, full-bleed 390x844 aspect ratio, no device frame.
Primary request: Fully redesigned reassuring, simple registration screen for Garde-manger, a shared household fridge app on iOS/Android. Art direction “Bienvenue chez vous”: contemporary invitation stationery with distinctive editorial typography and generous quiet space.
Color palette: flat warm cream #FFF9EF background, ink #272622, vermillion #B74432 accent, pale sand #E7D8C5. No gradients, brown panels, or card shell.
Composition: top system safe area with understated native status bar; small “Garde-manger” wordmark near top. Large expressive editorial serif headline on two lines “Bienvenue” then “chez vous.”, followed by smaller clean sans-serif “Un même garde-manger pour tout le foyer.” A tiny graceful illustrated still-life of exactly two pears in an enamel dish sits off to one side, secondary and not a hero/photo backdrop. Generous quiet space separates welcome and registration. Clear section heading “Créer mon compte”. Three full-width controls each about 50pt tall with ample tap targets and clear native legibility: “Continuer avec Apple”, “Continuer avec Google”, “Continuer avec e-mail”. Include appropriate small provider icons and a separator only where useful. Under buttons, readable “Déjà un compte ? Se connecter” with clearly actionable sign-in link. Quiet bottom row “Serveur officiel · Modifier” above bottom safe area and native home indicator.
Text verbatim: “Garde-manger”; “Bienvenue”; “chez vous.”; “Un même garde-manger pour tout le foyer.”; “Créer mon compte”; “Continuer avec Apple”; “Continuer avec Google”; “Continuer avec e-mail”; “Déjà un compte ? Se connecter”; “Serveur officiel · Modifier”.
Constraints: show one complete polished real app screen using an example server that enables all three providers. No security/free/trial claims, no extra marketing copy, no phone frame, no perspective, no panel behind the whole interface, no photos. Beautiful invitation-like hierarchy with legible French typography and consistent generous spacing.
